# Malaysia release gate G2 — Territory matrix and worker coverage

**Owner:** Product + Legal / Malaysian payroll specialist  
**Evidence tag:** `MY-GATE-G2` (a SourceArtifact reviewed by someone other than its uploader)  
**Spec rule:** Labour law is territorial (MY-001 / MY-024); a missing fact fails only its obligation (MY-008).

The gate passes only when every check below is met and the evidence is recorded. `malaysia_readiness.my_readiness()` re-derives this on every request; nothing is ticked by hand.

## Checks

- [ ] Every territorial labour row is signed for Peninsular + Labuan, Sabah and Sarawak (`territorial_rows_signed`): weekly hours, overtime exclusion wage, multipliers, ordinary-rate rule and divisor, leave tiers and day counts, leave-pay rule.
- [ ] The pay-period split rule for a territory transfer is signed (`my_transfer_split_rule`).
- [ ] The earning-classification matrix is complete and signed (`MY_EARNING_CLASS`, `earning_class_matrix_complete`) with no REVIEW treatment left (`no_review_treatments`).
- [ ] The release-1 scope is signed (`my_supported_contract_types`, `my_supported_pay_frequencies`).

## Evidence to record

The territory matrix review (Employment Act 1955, Sabah and Sarawak Labour Ordinances) and the coverage decision table for citizens, permanent residents and foreign workers.
