# Malaysia release gate G4 — PDPA and privacy

**Owner:** Security + Privacy  
**Evidence tag:** `MY-GATE-G4` (a SourceArtifact reviewed by someone other than its uploader)  
**Spec rule:** Identifiers are encrypted, masked and revealed only by a two-person, single-use request (MY-029).

The gate passes only when every check below is met and the evidence is recorded. `malaysia_readiness.my_readiness()` re-derives this on every request; nothing is ticked by hand.

## Checks

- [ ] `MY_IDENTIFIER_KEYRING` and `MY_IDENTIFIER_FINGERPRINT_KEY` are configured (presence only).
- [ ] Every identifier-vault row is under the newest key version (`vault_on_newest_key`).
- [ ] Retention periods are approved for payroll, tax and social-security records (owner decision D-8).

## Evidence to record

The PDPA role and transfer assessment, the DPO sign-off, the access matrix and the incident runbook.
