# Malaysia release gate G1 — Statutory content

**Owner:** Payroll Content + Malaysian payroll / tax specialist  
**Evidence tag:** `MY-GATE-G1` (a SourceArtifact reviewed by someone other than its uploader)  
**Spec rule:** NO-GO if any supported result relies on guessed or uncited content (spec page 1).

The gate passes only when every check below is met and the evidence is recorded. `malaysia_readiness.my_readiness()` re-derives this on every request; nothing is ticked by hand.

## Checks

- [ ] A canonical Malaysia pack is seeded (`pack_seeded`).
- [ ] No row carries `[PENDING G1]` (`no_pending_rows`): every PCB, EPF, PERKESO, EIS, HRD, minimum-wage, rounding and policy row has its value from an archived source reviewed by a second Super Admin. Undefined policies may be signed `NOT_SUPPORTED`, which keeps the feature blocked.
- [ ] The EPF Third Schedule, PERKESO, EIS and foreign-worker schedules and the public-holiday calendars are loaded and signed (`MY_EPF_SCHEDULE`, `MY_SOCSO_SCHEDULE`, `MY_EIS_SCHEDULE`, `MY_FW_SOCSO_SCHEDULE`, `MY_PUBLIC_HOLIDAY`).
- [ ] At least one official worked example is recorded and every expected figure matches the engine exactly (`authority_examples_match`).
- [ ] PCB is proven by at least one HASiL example (`pcb_proven`). This needs Phase 4 and the HASiL 2026 computerised specification with its official test questions.

## Evidence to record

The signed 2026 statutory register, the archived sources (S1-S15), the workbook files that loaded the schedules, and the worked examples with their provenance. The repository fixtures in `backend/tests/_my_engine_helpers.py` are NOT certification fixtures: they use TEST values.
