# Poland release gate G7 — Operations and commercial

**Owner:** Payroll Ops + Product / Management  
**Evidence tag:** `PL-GATE-G7` (a SourceArtifact reviewed by someone other than its uploader)  
**Spec rule:** NO-GO if operational ownership or market claims exceed the certified scope.

The gate passes only when every check below is met and the evidence is recorded. `poland_readiness.pl_readiness()` re-derives this on every request (Super Admin → Poland → Release gates, or the `readiness` block of `GET /api/payroll/poland/summary`); nothing is ticked by hand.

## Checks

- [ ] Retention periods are approved for PAYROLL_RECORDS, TAX_RECORDS and SOCIAL_INSURANCE_RECORDS (owner decision D-7).
- [ ] The four sign-offs `PL-SIGNOFF-PRODUCT`, `PL-SIGNOFF-COMPLIANCE`, `PL-SIGNOFF-ENGINEERING` and `PL-SIGNOFF-COMMERCIAL` are recorded.

## Evidence to record

The statutory calendar, support runbooks ([`../RUNBOOK_GO_LIVE.md`](../RUNBOOK_GO_LIVE.md)), launch employer(s), contractual scope and country claims.
