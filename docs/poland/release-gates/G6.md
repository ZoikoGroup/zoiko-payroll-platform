# Poland release gate G6 — Parallel payroll

**Owner:** QA + Implementation  
**Evidence tag:** `PL-GATE-G6` (a SourceArtifact reviewed by someone other than its uploader)  
**Spec rule:** NO-GO on unexplained material monetary or statutory variance.

The gate passes only when every check below is met and the evidence is recorded. `poland_readiness.pl_readiness()` re-derives this on every request (Super Admin → Poland → Release gates, or the `readiness` block of `GET /api/payroll/poland/summary`); nothing is ticked by hand.

## Checks

- [ ] At least 2 legal employers each have ACCEPTED parallel cycles in at least 2 different pay months (`parallel_cycles`, AC-PL-03).
- [ ] Each cycle compares the current provider's figures with Zoiko's frozen payslips at zero tolerance; every difference is explained and accepted by a second person; the cycle is accepted by someone other than its importer.

## Evidence to record

The accepted cycles (Compliance Centre → Parallel cycles) and the signed variance report.
