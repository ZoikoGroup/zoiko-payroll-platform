# New Zealand release gate G5 — Parallel cycles accepted

**Owner:** Payroll operations  
**Evidence tag:** `NZ-GATE-G5` (a SourceArtifact reviewed by someone other than its uploader)

The gate passes only when every check below is met and the evidence is recorded. `nz_readiness.nz_readiness()` re-derives this on every request (Super Admin → New Zealand → Release gates, or `GET /api/payroll/new-zealand/readiness`); nothing is ticked by hand.

## Checks

- [ ] At least 2 legal employers each have ACCEPTED parallel cycles in at least 2 different pay months (`parallel_cycles`, AC-NZ-03).
- [ ] Each cycle compares the current provider's figures with Zoiko's frozen payslips at zero tolerance; every difference explained and accepted by a second person; the cycle accepted by someone other than its importer.

## Evidence to record

The accepted cycles (visible in the Compliance Centre) and the operations lead's sign-off.
